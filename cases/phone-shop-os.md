# Phone Shop OS — Iraqi retail operations platform

**Domain:** POS, inventory, device tracking, installments and business processes  
**Stage:** Significant tested implementation in an active development branch; not commercially released

## Problem

Phone and electronics retailers must link stock, device identities, transactions, customer balances, installments and accounting. Separate or inaccurate records increase mistakes and operational work.

## Approach

- Built a multi-tenant business software architecture.
- Implemented development checkpoints for inventory and IMEI tracking, customer and point-of-sale flows, installments and account records.
- Added automated verification of data, role and workflow behavior.
- Worked on regional payment-adapter boundaries without suggesting that a provider-approved live gateway was already active.

## Honest maturity

**Technical implementation and tests are not the same as a paid retail rollout.** A Qi-like gateway adapter was tested with simulated or mock integrations; live payment certification and recurring customer revenue are not claimed.

## Founder lesson

Building broad features before sustained customer observation creates commercialization risk. Next gate: tightly scoped pilots, real user feedback and validation of price and support requirements.
