# Research directions & future collaboration

I am interested in graduate research and practical collaborations at the intersection of:

1. **Robotics and intelligent control** — cyber-physical systems, mechatronics, embedded control and real-world robot simulation.
2. **Trustworthy AI systems** — evidence-bounded retrieval, reproducibility, safe operator interfaces and model reliability under incomplete data.
3. **Industrial AI / BMS / IoT** — sensors, supervisory monitoring and interoperable control systems with human oversight.
4. **Resilient software** — secure multi-tenant applications, distributed data, offline-first mobile engineering and human-centered workflows.

## Distinguishing completed work from ambition

- **Implemented/field-tested evidence:** Syntra read-only integration; verified Mathematics SADS application; publicly accessible Al-Ghazaly Atlas; deployed Tally pilot infrastructure.
- **Tested but incomplete field acceptance:** Wasl and Phone Shop OS.
- **Research direction, not a validated robot:** independent robotics and 3D simulation experiments.
- **Business ambition, not established revenue:** Telos Nexus is developing and validating a commercial software model in Iraq.
